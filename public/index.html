Get your data from Google Maps

Google doesn't let apps read your saved places directly, so you export them yourself. It takes about two minutes.

  • 1

    Open Google Takeout

    Go to takeout.google.com, signed in to the Google account with your saved places.

  • 2

    Select only "Saved"

    Click Deselect all, then scroll down and check Saved — your Google Maps lists. Leave the format as CSV.

    🔖
    SavedCollections of saved links (images, places, web pages, etc.) from Google Search and Maps.
    ✓
  • 3

    Create the export

    Click Next step, keep the "email a download link" option, then Create export. It's usually ready in a few minutes.

  • 4

    Download and unzip

    Open the emailed link, download the .zip, and unzip it. Inside a Saved folder you'll find one CSV per list, named after each list.

  • 5

    Upload the trip you want

    Drop the CSV for that trip into Pinbook, on the right. That's it.
